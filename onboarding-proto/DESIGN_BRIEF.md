# B0–B8 design brief

Post-install onboarding for ProductAgent. Public vocabulary is only **Loop**, **Run**,
**Order**, **Decision**, plus plain English. Internal words (lane, envelope, wrapper,
standup, seed, root) stay off this surface.

Tone: a closed Mac at 2am. Deep navy, cream type, moonlight gold. Not a neon deck.

Demo only. No real install. No network.

## B0 — Cold boot

Black-navy terminal. Cursor. The machine lands in `~/loops`.

Show, in order: fence loaded; battery floor 30%; AC gate (wait until plugged in);
schedule armed; `loops: 0`; **nothing runs until you write a loop**.

End on a waiting caret. Enter begins.

## B1 — Lid promise

Full-bleed night: crescent, mist, mountains. The product sentence, once:

> Let an agent run a loop while you sleep.

Lid shut. Same Mac. Same Claude plan. Three facts: eight free hours; lid shut in the
bag; runs on what you already pay for. Fine print lives in the facts, not a modal:
plugged in, never under thirty percent battery.

## B2 — Four-noun loadout

Four slots. Keyboard `1`–`4`. Each slot is a word, a gloss, and a short body.

- **Loop** — a goal that keeps renewing. You write it; the agent never can.
- **Run** — one bounded pass, tonight, under a budget, inside the fence.
- **Order** — a one-off for tonight. Orders go first; Loops fill the rest.
- **Decision** — what the Run will not guess at. Hands it back in the morning.

Continue only after all four are armed.

## B3 — First Loop creation

Write one Loop. Pick from the six site starters or edit the goal. Four knobs: goal,
budget, cadence, model. Live markdown preview of the `pm/LOOPS.md` section. Status is
`draft`. First three Runs you review. Confirm with Enter.

## B4 — Overnight time-lapse

Lid closes. Clock runs 23:12 → 06:48. A short log: plugged in, fence, L-01 Run,
work inside the fence, four lines written, machine sleeping. Esc skips. Reduced
motion: still frame of the finished night.

## B5 — Morning report

The four sentences, in the product’s own shape:

```
## L-01
- Trying to:
- Did:
- Decided:
- Need from you:
```

If the four lines are missing, the run is not done. The last line is a Decision.
Mark it seen.

## B6 — Karma tease

A quiet constellation, not a neon graph. Reports and a Decision as nodes. Copy:
a month of reports stays searchable. Optional. After a month of mornings. Open source.

## B7 — Phone tease

A phone frame. Telegram as the second door. `/loops`. `/loop L-01 budget 5`. This
morning’s four lines. Every edit lands in `pm/LOOPS.md`, dated and marked as yours.

## B8 — You’re live

Recap the four words against the Loop they wrote. Plug the laptop in tonight. Replay.
Fine print once more. Nothing phones home.

## Success

A stranger who has never seen ProductAgent can finish this in 60–90 seconds and tell
you what a Loop, a Run, an Order, and a Decision are, and why the lid can close.
